Ask · the heart of Selah

Ask anything. Get a real answer, in writing.

This is the part nothing else does. Submit an honest question about God, doubt, scripture, suffering, or life — and a legitimate one gets a full written answer: AI-drafted from scripture and trusted sources, then reviewed and edited by a person before it’s published. It joins a growing, searchable library so the next person finds it too.

✍️

1 · You ask

A short, warm form. Add context if you like. Submit anonymously if you’d rather — no judgment.

🧭

2 · We draft

AI reads it, finds what’s already been answered, and drafts a grounded, cited response from an approved corpus.

🪶

3 · A person reviews

An editor checks every answer against scripture and our stated beliefs before it’s ever published.

📚

4 · It’s saved

The answer becomes a permanent, searchable library entry — and you’re notified yours is ready.

How this works in detail — including the safeguards — is written up in the AI Q&A pipeline doc.

Ask your question
