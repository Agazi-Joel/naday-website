Where your money goes

We believe generosity should be transparent. Our intended model:

  • ✦ The majority of every gift funds actual Bibles via a distribution partner
  • ✦ The split (Bibles vs. processing vs. overhead) is published openly
  • ✦ Subscriptions — not gifts — cover the platform’s running costs

The exact fulfillment partner and the transparency model are still open decisions.
