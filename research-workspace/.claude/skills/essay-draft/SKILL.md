# Essay draft

Turns an **approved** structure into a working draft she rewrites in her own voice.
Read the integrity rules in `knowledge/workspace-conventions.md` before using this.

## Preconditions
- An approved outline (`argument-builder`) exists.
- Notes and `papers/3-to-cite/` are in place for the evidence.

## Steps
1. Confirm the outline and thesis with her first.
2. Draft section by section, following the outline. Every claim that needs support
   carries an in-text Harvard cite drawn from a **real** source in her notes/citing
   folder. Build the reference list from cited sources only.
3. Match the word budget per section.

## Output (clearly labelled)
- Save to `outputs/<assignment>-draft-vN.md`.
- Put a banner at the top:
  `> WORKING DRAFT — rewrite in your own voice before submission. Verify every citation.`
- Mark any spot where evidence is missing as `[NEEDS SOURCE]` rather than inventing one.
- Mark reflective/first-person passages as `[YOUR REFLECTION — write this yourself]`.

## Rules (non-negotiable)
- This is scaffolding, **not** submittable text. Do not present it as finished.
- Never fabricate references, quotes, or page numbers.
- Reflective and personal-judgement passages are placeholders for **her** to write.
- After drafting, recommend running `supervisor-critique` on it.
