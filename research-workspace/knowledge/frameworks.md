# Frameworks & theory library

A reference of models these courses lean on, so `argument-builder`,
`theory-to-practice` and `supervisor-critique` can name, apply and *critique* them
rather than describing them blandly. For each: the gist, plus a critique hook
(where it's challenged) — because critical evaluation is what earns marks.

> This is a primer, not a substitute for reading the primary sources. Always cite
> the original author, not this file.

## Leadership

- **Transformational vs transactional (Bass, Burns)** — inspiring/visionary vs
  exchange-based. *Critique:* heroic/individualist; weak on context; measurement
  issues; can mask coercion.
- **Collective & compassionate leadership (West)** — leadership distributed across
  all, built on compassion and inclusion; strongly promoted in the NHS. *Critique:*
  can underplay accountability, power and hard trade-offs under financial pressure.
- **System / collaborative leadership** — leading across organisational boundaries
  (ICSs/ICBs). *Critique:* diffuse accountability; slow; depends on relationships
  that outlast reorganisations.
- **Servant leadership (Greenleaf)** — leader serves followers first. *Critique:*
  evidence base thinner; risk of role conflict and burnout.
- **Situational leadership (Hersey & Blanchard)** — adapt style to follower
  readiness. *Critique:* limited empirical support; oversimplified.
- **Authentic / distributed / complexity leadership** — useful contemporary lenses;
  note their critiques (vagueness, hard to operationalise).
- **NHS Leadership Model / Healthcare Leadership Model** — the sector's own
  competency framework; useful as applied context.

## Change & improvement

- **Lewin** — unfreeze / change / refreeze. *Critique:* too linear for complex,
  continuous change.
- **Kotter's 8 steps** — popular roadmap. *Critique:* top-down, prescriptive,
  weak evidence base, assumes stability.
- **ADKAR** — individual-level change. *Critique:* underplays system/context.
- **Quality Improvement / PDSA, Model for Improvement, Lean/Six Sigma** — core NHS
  improvement methods. *Critique:* implementation gap; sustainability; can become
  ritualistic.
- **Complexity / systems thinking** — health systems as complex adaptive systems.
  *Critique:* powerful framing but hard to translate into action.

## Organisation & culture

- **Schein — organisational culture** (artefacts/values/assumptions).
- **Just culture / safety culture** (Reason's Swiss cheese; psychological safety —
  Edmondson).
- **Institutional & professional dynamics** — clinical vs managerial logics, a
  recurring tension in NHS management.
