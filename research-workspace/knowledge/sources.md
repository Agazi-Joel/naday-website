# Sources

Where to look for evidence in healthcare leadership & management. Discovery skills
(`field-survey`, `journal-finder`, `paper-search`) read this so they search the
right places — including grey literature, which is essential in this field.

## Academic databases / search engines

- **PubMed / MEDLINE** — clinical + health services research. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
- **CINAHL** — nursing & allied health (via her university library login).
- **HMIC** (Health Management Information Consortium) — UK health management &
  policy, incl. King's Fund and DH grey literature (via library).
- **Semantic Scholar** — broad, good for citation graphs. https://www.semanticscholar.org/
- **OpenAlex** — open metadata, good for mapping a field. https://openalex.org/
- **Google Scholar** — broad sweep + "cited by" tracing.
- **Business Source / ABI-INFORM** — management & organisational behaviour (via library).

Always remind her to use her **university library** login — it unlocks paywalled
full text the open tools only show abstracts for.

## Grey literature (high value in health management)

- **The King's Fund** — https://www.kingsfund.org.uk/
- **Nuffield Trust** — https://www.nuffieldtrust.org.uk/
- **The Health Foundation** — https://www.health.org.uk/
- **NHS England** — strategy, Long Term Plan, leadership frameworks.
- **CQC** — regulation, quality, State of Care reports.
- **NICE** — guidance and evidence standards.
- **NHS Confederation**, **NHS Providers**, **The Care Quality Commission**.
- **WHO**, **OECD Health** — international comparison.

Treat these as citable evidence (organisation as author) — see `citation-style.md`.

## Search hygiene

- Combine a **topic term** with a **field/context term**, e.g.
  `"transformational leadership" AND (nursing OR NHS OR "health care")`.
- Prefer recent (last ~5–10 years) for policy/context; seminal works can be older.
- Always capture: authors, year, title, journal/source, DOI or URL — needed for citing.
- Never present a source as found/verified unless it actually resolves to a real
  record. Flag anything uncertain.
