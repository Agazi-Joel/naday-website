## What these assignments reward

Management master's marking usually rewards, in roughly this order:
1. **Critical evaluation** — not "what does the model say" but "where does it hold,
   where does it break, who disagrees, what's the evidence."
2. **Application to practice** — theory mapped onto a real NHS situation, with
   nuance about why it fits or doesn't.
3. **Use of evidence** — a balance of peer-reviewed literature *and* credible grey
   literature (King's Fund, Nuffield Trust, etc.).
4. **Structure and argument** — a clear thesis carried through, not a description.
5. **Reflection** (where asked) — honest, structured, in her own voice.

A common failure mode is *description over analysis*. Skills should push toward
critique and application.

## Recurring themes in NHS management study

System/integrated leadership (ICSs, ICBs) · compassionate & collective leadership ·
workforce, retention & burnout · quality and patient safety · change management in
complex systems · culture and behaviour · equality/diversity/inclusion · the
NHS Long Term Plan and policy context · financial pressure and value · regulation
(CQC) and accountability.

## Common reflective models (for reflective assignments)

Gibbs' reflective cycle · Kolb's experiential learning · Driscoll (What? So what?
Now what?) · Rolfe et al. Keep reflection in her voice — see integrity rules.
