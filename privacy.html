This Privacy Policy explains how Naday Tech, a trading name of the Naday Global Group ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you interact with our website, our products, or our services.

We believe that the products we build — in social care, heritage, language, and education — are only as trustworthy as the way we handle the data they touch. This policy is intended to be readable, specific, and honest.

1. Who we are

Naday Tech is the technology arm of the Naday Global Group. Where this policy refers to "we" or "Naday Tech", it refers to the legal entity operating under that trading name, whose contact details are at the end of this document.

2. What we collect

We only collect information that we need to operate our website, deliver our products, and meet our legal obligations. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Contact information — your name, email address, and any message you send us when you get in touch.
  • Account information — if you create an account with one of our products, the credentials and profile information needed to operate it.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as your device type, operating system, IP address, and the pages or features you use, collected for security and product improvement.
  • Product-specific data — any information you choose to add to one of our products (for example, family records in a heritage app or learning progress in an education app).

3. How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate, and improve our website and products;
  • respond to enquiries and provide support;
  • protect our services from fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests;
  • communicate with you about updates, where you have asked us to or where we are permitted to do so.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising profiling.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only in limited circumstances:

  • with trusted service providers who help us operate our products (for example, cloud hosting, analytics, email delivery), under contracts that require them to protect your data;
  • with other companies in the Naday Global Group, where this is necessary to provide the service you have requested;
  • where required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. AI and automated processing

Several of our products use artificial intelligence to deliver features such as language understanding, personalisation, and content generation. Where AI processing involves personal data, we use providers and configurations that, to the best of our knowledge at the time of processing, do not train their foundation models on your inputs. Specific AI behaviour is documented per product.

6. Storage and security

We hold personal information on infrastructure operated by reputable providers and apply industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; if we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities in line with applicable law.

7. International transfers

As a group with international reach, we may transfer personal information across borders. Where we do, we use lawful transfer mechanisms (such as standard contractual clauses) to maintain the protection of your data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal information, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address at the end of this policy. We will respond within the timeframe required by law.

9. Children

Some of our products are designed for use in educational and family contexts that may include minors. Where this is the case, the relevant product will have its own age-appropriate privacy notice and parental controls. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children outside of those contexts.

10. Cookies

Our website uses a minimal set of cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality and aggregate analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

12. Contact

For any privacy questions, or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@nadaytech.com.

For general enquiries: hello@nadaytech.com.
